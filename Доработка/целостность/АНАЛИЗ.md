# Design coherence — analysis (2026-10-07)

User's note: after many edits the site looks assembled from pieces and drifts from ore in places.
They like the first three blocks (hero, «Форматы», 01 «Подготовка»); after that style and the premium feel are lost.

Captured: site at 1440×900 (`листы/sheet0–5.png`, script `листы/shots.py`), ore (`листы/ref0–2.png`).
The site matches Figma (comparisons ≤ 2.2 px) → the problem is the design, not the build.

## What holds the first three blocks

- The voice is a giant full-width grotesk (БАЛЫ, РАЗГОВОР / КОНЦЕПЦИЯ / …); the serif phrase only leads into it.
- One screen — one idea, 1–2 caps fragments, lots of empty space.
- Photo — full-bleed band or full screen, word on top.
- Colour — the screen's surface (ink / terra), not lines or labels.
- Each block has its own composition.

## Where it breaks (02–12)

1. **The giant pole is gone.** From 02 to 12 the main voice is a serif heading ~100–110. Giants only in 05 (show) and 10 (ОМЕГА БАЛ).
   ore has a giant or a full-bleed photo on every screen.
2. **One header template ×10:** label «NN — РАЗДЕЛ», 2-line serif ladder, caps paragraph on the right.
   Identical headers + dissimilar bodies = reads as a template with random inserts.
3. **Bodies became UI / infographics:** timeline table with accent rules (02), three card columns (03), ruled list (06),
   four step columns (07), tab buttons and a numbered list on a beige plate (08), captions with crosshairs around photos — callouts (09),
   collage of four photos of different sizes (10).
4. **Middle sizes:** serif lines 30–40 (timeline items, 06 quote, Omega lead, «О нас», phone/email ~50),
   grotesk 20–30 (03, 06 items, «Расскажите о вашем вечере») — breaks the two poles.
5. **Density:** 3–4 caps paragraphs per screen in a ladder (08, 09) plus labels and numbers — no empty space.
6. **Patchwork colour:** surfaces change inside sections (grey bands at the bottom of 06, 07, 08; photo band above the next header).
   Terracotta went decorative — rules, numbers, labels, a word on a plate.
7. **Photos differ in tone and format:** b/w in 06, warm toning in 04, natural elsewhere; bands, cards, collage.
8. **Weak ending:** 11 «О нас» (432 px) and 12 «Связаться» (280 px) — thin strips, no giant.
   ore ends with a giant LET'S TELL YOUR STORY + serif phrase + «→ contact us».
9. **Looks like a bug:** 04 «Танцмейстер» — the «Зрители / Участники» captions are clipped by the photo edge at rest (`листы/crop_dm.png`).

## Screen library (draft — built in Ц1)

- **A** — giant word over a full-bleed photo (hero, 05).
- **B** — giant split across two surfaces («Форматы»).
- **C** — serif phrase + giant list (01 «Подготовка»).
- **D** — one photo object centred, a giant overlapping it (ore BRIDAL / CUSTOM).
- **E** — full-width giant + 1–3 caps fragments on the axes (ore PROJECTS / CUSTOM).
- **F** — finale: giant + serif phrase + «→ link» (ore contact).

## Rules

- every screen has a giant (≥ 150) or a full-bleed photo;
- serif — one phrase per section, not a header template;
- no middle sizes;
- caps — at most three fragments per screen;
- no rules, tables, tabs or callouts;
- terracotta — only a surface or one giant word;
- one photo tone across the site.
