## Д5 — Header: hides and shading (П-09, Р-5)

1. Hides on scrolling down, appears on scrolling up, at all widths; always visible at the top of the page and with the menu open;
   where else it doesn't hide — decide with the user after Д2–Д3 (jumps to anchors from the menu, pinned scenes 02 and 05, the form sheet).
2. A soft gradient shade under the header in all blocks (as in the hero); over light panels (08, form) — no shade.
3. Rewrite `header.js` together with «Над светлым» and the Р-5 fix (re-check zones after an entrance ends). reduced-motion — no slide animation.
4. A development note in Figma (header behaviour), header autotests.

**Д5 order — trial, "code first" (user decision 2026-10-07, to save tokens):**

- Don't build variants in Figma. The forks from items 1–2 (where the header doesn't hide, shade strength and height, slide speed) —
  first in words with a recommendation, then variants **directly on the site via a switch** `?v=A` / `?v=B` (an attribute on `<html>`, read by CSS and `header.js`).
- The user reviews the variants in Chrome (`tools/serve.py`, desktop and device mode). Claude doesn't eyeball renders.
- After "yes" — remove the switch and the rejected variant from the code; the mechanics (autotests, regression) can go to the `executor` agent.
- Figma — at the end, one note on header behaviour next to the live frames; no header state frames.
- Afterwards — a short verdict in ЖУРНАЛ: was the order convenient, what came out cheaper or worse. This decides whether to switch to it for Д6 and stages 6–7.
